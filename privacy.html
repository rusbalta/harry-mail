Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Harry Mail is a personal tool. I, Nal Hober, am its only user, and it connects only my own Google accounts.

What it reads

With read-only access to Gmail, it reads the messages in each connected account: sender, recipients, subject, date, the short preview Gmail shows, and the full text of a message when I open it.

What it does with it

It shows my mail in my own app on my phone and laptop, and each morning my assistant lists the mail I have not answered yet. My assistant runs on Claude, so the details it reads (subject, date and the sender with the address masked) are sent to Anthropic to produce that list. Nothing else happens with the data. It is not sold, not used for ads and not shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

The access token and the message details stay on my own computer, in files only my user account can read, and in my own app's cache on my devices. They are left out of every backup.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account, under Third-party apps and services. From then on the tool reads nothing more from that account, and I delete its saved token.
