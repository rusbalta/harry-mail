Bank data privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Harry & Steve is a personal tool. I, Nal Hober, am its only user. Through Enable Banking it connects only my own accounts at OTP banka and N26, with read-only account information access. It cannot make payments.

What it keeps

For each account: the bank, the account or Space name, the last 4 characters of the IBAN and the balance. For each transaction from the last 400 days: the date, the amount, a cleaned merchant name for payments and the bank's category code.

Holder names, addresses, payment descriptions, full account and card numbers are dropped as soon as the data arrives and are never stored or logged. Full IBANs are kept only as a one-way hash.

What it does with it

It shows my balances and spending in my own app on my phone and laptop. My assistant gets my totals, last month's spending by category and saving tips, without IBANs or names. The assistant runs on Claude, so those figures are sent to Anthropic to produce its answers. Nothing else happens with the data. It is not sold, not used for ads and not shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

On my own computer, in files only my user account can read, and in my own app's cache on my devices. It is left out of every backup.

Removing access

Each bank consent lasts at most 180 days. I can end it sooner by deleting the session at Enable Banking, and I can delete the stored data at any time.
