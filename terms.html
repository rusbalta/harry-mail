Harry & Steve is a personal tool that I, Nal Hober, built for myself. It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up and costs nothing.

It reads my own bank accounts through Enable Banking with account information access only. It cannot start payments or move money.

Balances, categories and tips it shows are for my own overview. My bank's records are the ones that count.

How the bank data is handled is in the bank data privacy policy.
